# Asado Manager Privacy Policy

[Versión en español](../)

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

Asado Manager ("the app") is an app for organizing barbecues (asados) and
splitting the costs among the guests. This policy explains what information the
app uses, what Google does with the ads it shows, and what choices you have.

**Controller:** Peach Dev

**Contact:** peachdev1899@gmail.com

## 1. Summary

- The information you enter (guests, amounts, tags) **is stored only on your
  device**. There are no accounts or developer servers, and the developer does
  not receive or see that data.
- The app shows **Google AdMob ads**. For that, Google may collect some device
  data, as explained in section 5.
- The app does not use your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or
  photos.

## 2. Information you enter in the app

While using the app you can enter: barbecue titles and dates, the names of the
guests, the amounts each one spent, which categories they took part in, and
"who brings what" tags.

This information:

- is stored **only in your device's local storage**;
- is not sent to the developer or to any server of the app;
- is kept until you delete it inside the app (for example, by deleting a
  barbecue) or uninstall the app.

## 3. Sharing feature

The share button builds a text summary (total, how much each one pays and who
pays whom) and hands it to your operating system's share menu. You choose which
app and with whom to share it. The app does not see or control where it goes.
Keep in mind that the summary includes the names and amounts you entered.

## 4. Local storage of preferences

The app stores two numeric counters on your device, only to decide how many
actions to wait before showing a full-screen ad, and the language you choose
inside the app. They contain no personal data.

## 5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK). When ads are
shown, Google may collect and use, among other data:

- your device's advertising identifier (Android advertising ID or IDFA on iOS);
- your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
- device and system information (model, version, language, connection type);
- data about how you interact with ads.

Google uses it to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, and
acts as an independent controller of that information. More information:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Consent and choices.**

- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app
  shows a consent form (based on Google's User Messaging Platform) before
  requesting ads. Ads are only requested according to your choice. In those
  countries, the app's main screen shows a "Privacy preferences" button (a
  shield with an "i") so you can review or change that choice at any time.
- On iOS, the app asks for your permission to track you (App Tracking
  Transparency). If you decline, you will see non-personalized ads.
- You can reset or limit the advertising identifier from your device settings
  (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security >
  Tracking).

## 6. System backups

Your operating system (Android or iOS) may include the app's data in the backup
of your Google or Apple account, if you have it turned on. That backup is
managed by Google or Apple under their own policies; the developer does not
access it.

## 7. Data the app does not collect

The app has no user registration, does not use analytics or crash reporting
services, and does not request access to your precise location, contacts,
calendar, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## 8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in the European Union,
and does not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child
has used the app, you can write to the contact above.

## 9. Your rights

Since the developer does not store your data, you control it directly: you can
view, edit and delete all the information inside the app, or remove it entirely
by uninstalling it. For the data Google processes for advertising purposes, you
can exercise your rights (access, rectification, deletion, objection) with
Google, through your ads settings and the links in section 5. For any question
about this policy, write to the contact above.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address
and the date at the top will be updated.
